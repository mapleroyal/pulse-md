# Third-party notices

Pulse MD includes third-party software and font assets under their own licenses.
Those licenses apply to the relevant third-party material independently of the
license covering Pulse MD's own source.

Vite's built-in license generator writes the notices for software in the
production renderer bundle to `licenses/third-party.json`. Electron's license
and its upstream Chromium credits are copied intact to `licenses/electron`.

This hand-maintained supplement covers software bundled into the Electron main
process outside Vite's renderer graph, bundled assets, and derived UI source
that the generated renderer notices cannot discover.

## Electron main-process ZIP dependencies

The production Electron main-process bundle includes `yauzl` 3.4.0 and its
transitive dependency `pend` 1.2.0, plus `yazl` 3.3.1 and its transitive
dependency `buffer-crc32` 1.0.0.
